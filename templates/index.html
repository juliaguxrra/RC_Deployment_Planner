How It Works

  • Your choices: Uses the ship, date, trip length, and planning priority you select.
  • Route match: Uses a matching published Royal Caribbean route when available. Otherwise, it creates a labeled sample route.
  • Basic checks: Compares ship draft, estimated port space, and expected guest volume.
  • Route details: Calculates distance and counts port days and days at sea.
  • Plan score: Uses estimated experience, cost, guest spending, and distance.
  • Recommendation: Applies your priority, ranks the options, and recommends the best match.

Routes come from Royal Caribbean’s public website. Draft, port capacity, costs, guest spending, and experience scores are estimates I created for this prototype. Gemini explains the recommendation; the scoring model chooses it.
